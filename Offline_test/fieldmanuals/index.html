    The Field Manuals (FM)

    • FM01: Game Operations

      Detailed information on the game's fundamentals, including navigating its interface, and in-depth looks at the systems.

    • FM02: Battlefield Primer

      We try to impart lessons learned from the game and those elements of real-world tactics that apply and can save your backside in this game.

    • FM03A: Basic Tutorial Operations

      Introduces you to hands-on tutorials designed to help you learn the basics of key battlefield operations

    • FM03B: Intermediate Tutorial Operations

      Guiding you through the Tutorial Scenarios for conducting Engineering, Artillery, Air Support, and NBC operations.

    • FM03C: Advanced Tutorial Operations

      Guiding you through the Tutorial Scenarios for planning Air Assault and Convoy operations.

    • Hotkeys

      Reference guide for the keyboard shortcuts and corresponding functions used in game.

    Note

    PDF format of these documents are found in the Documents\FMs folder.
