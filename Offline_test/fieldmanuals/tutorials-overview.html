    FM03 Tutorials Overview

    Basic Tutorial

    • User Interface Layout

      Learn the layout of the game’s interface and the functions of several useful overlays and dialogs to help with your planning and battle.

    • Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

      Learn how to set and adjust the Standard Operation Procedures (SOPs) for your units to make them fight and survive on the battlefield.

    • Issuing Orders To Units

      Learn how to issue orders to move units, call in support strikes, and rest and refit your force.

    • Command and Control

      Learn how the chain of command and condition of your units impacts orders delay and other functions.

    • Resolving the Battle

      Turn by turn through the tutorial.

    • End Game Post-Mortem

      Learn how review the various dialogs that explain the end of game scoring and resulting losses and expenditures of both forces.

    Intermediate Tutorials

    • Engineering Operations

      Learn how combat engineers can clear obstacles and mines, place demolitions to destroy bridges, and build defensive structures.

    • Artillery Operations

      Learn how to call in and control artillery fire, supporting friendly units with powerful indirect fire.

    • Air Support Operations

      Learn how to request and coordinate airstrikes, providing your ground forces with a critical edge in combat.

    • NBC Operations

      Learn how to request and coordinate strikes with Nuclear and Chemical weapons.

    Advanced Tutorials

    • Air Assault Operations

      Learn how to plan and execute helicopter-based missions to deploy troops into critical battlefield positions rapidly.

    • Convoy Operations

      Practice moving and protecting convoys while learning how to deal with potential ambushes and road threats.
