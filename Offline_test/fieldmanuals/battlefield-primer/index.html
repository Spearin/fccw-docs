    1.1 What's in This Document

    This document describes basic information and the use of the various unit types, weapon types, sensors, and systems found in the game. We will do our best to provide real-world service and in-game details of how they work or what they do. We provide some of the basic "dos and don'ts" of modern tactical warfare, but not an army training manual deep. We try to impart lessons learned from the game and those elements of real-world tactics that apply and can save your backside in this game.
