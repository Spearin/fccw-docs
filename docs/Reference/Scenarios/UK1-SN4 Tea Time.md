# UK1-SN4 Tea Time
_D+5 | 25 July 1989, 0230hrs - 1030hrs [:smflag-gb:](./Forces/United Kingdom.html) vs [:smflag-su:](./Forces/Soviet Union.html) @ [Unna](../Maps/Unna/Unna.html)_

The Warsaw Pact drive crossed the Teutoburg Wald at its western boundary, and rapidly advanced south towards the Ruhr. NATO commanders are throwing all available reserves into trying to shore up the front lines. As part of that effort, the brigade is ordered to attack the vital road network around the city of Unna. Despite the grumblings of the troops promised their afternoon tea, the attack would commence on schedule.
