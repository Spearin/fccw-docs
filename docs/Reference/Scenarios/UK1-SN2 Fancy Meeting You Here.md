# UK1-SN2 Fancy Meeting You Here
_D+0 | 20 July 1989, 1900hrs - 0100hrs [:smflag-gb:](./Forces/United Kingdom.html) [:smflag-de:](./Forces/West Germany.html) vs [:smflag-su:](./Forces/Soviet Union.html) @ [Holle](../Maps/Holle/Holle.html)_

While the 2nd Royal Tank Regiment is delaying around Wolfenbuttel, the remainder of the brigade is hastily ordered to the Holle area. 12 Armoured Bde normally assigned that sector have not arrived. It is feared elements of the Soviet 10th Guards Tank Division are racing to secure the vital road junction.
