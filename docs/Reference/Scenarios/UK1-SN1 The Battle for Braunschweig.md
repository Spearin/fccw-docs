# UK1-SN1 The Battle for Braunschweig
_D+0 | 20 July 1989, 1000hrs - 1500hrs [:smflag-gb:](./Forces/United Kingdom.html) vs [:smflag-su:](./Forces/Soviet Union.html) @ [Braunschweig](../Maps/Braunschweig/Braunschweig.html)_

The Third World War has begun, and Warsaw Pact armies are pouring across the Inner German Border. Elements of the famed 7th Armoured Brigade, along with recce troops from the British I Corps Screening Force, are tasked with slowing down the lead tanks of the Soviet 7th Guards Tank Division. The brigade has assigned a battlegroup based around the 2nd Royal Tank Regiment with the task of delaying any Soviet attack south of Braunschweig.
